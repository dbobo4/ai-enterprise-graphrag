# Incident report — October 2026 manifest rejection

On 2026-10-27, Project Atlas Archive rejected a pilot document batch. The root cause was an incompatible date format in the batch manifest. The incident is closed. The package reached the intake area, but its delivery dates could not be accepted by the manifest parser. The paper envelopes remained available in the quarantine cabinet.

The operator had copied dates from a local spreadsheet export that used a different day and month arrangement. Page counts and receipt number ranges were present. Support retained the rejected manifest and compared it with the approved sample package. The team stopped further copies of the same export until the intake coordinator confirmed which packages had been prepared with that template.

Recovery corrected the date format and resubmitted the batch under its original intake reference. The reviewer compared the accepted package with the paper envelopes before releasing it for storage. A new delivery attempt did not create another retention start event. The rejection entry was closed only after both the manifest receipt and the page inspection were recorded.

The corrective action adds a local preview of dates before an operator sends a manifest. A preview must display an unambiguous year, month and day rather than relying on spreadsheet formatting. The scanning desk will keep the approved export settings with its operating instructions. A changed spreadsheet template requires another pilot package before routine use.

Operations will review the remaining rehearsal packages before the next migration window. The incident did not authorise a wider transfer of active dispute records. The programme coordinator has reserved another inspection slot for the same sample group. Any envelope with a damaged signature page remains quarantined under the normal readability process, independently of this manifest issue.
