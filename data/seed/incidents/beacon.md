# Incident report — August 2026 switch interruption

On 2026-08-25, Project Beacon recorded intermittent office network disconnections. The root cause was faulty firmware on a replacement network switch. The incident is closed. Reception and the west meeting room experienced repeated connection loss during the afternoon equipment check, while the north office cabinet remained stable.

The engineer first inspected power and patch connections because both affected areas shared the replacement device. A temporary cable change did not remove the fault. Comparing the event sequence with the installation sheet showed that the replacement was running a firmware release outside the accepted set. The team retained the event log before starting the rollback.

Recovery restored the approved firmware and repeated the connection test from both affected areas. The reception printer was checked separately because its queue had paused during the interruption. The facilities coordinator asked staff to report any remaining desk problem before moving cables again. The engineer closed the observation window after the next working morning's check.

The corrective action requires the installed firmware version to be recorded before a device joins an office segment. A service sheet without that information is incomplete even if its serial number matches the delivery note. Procurement will keep acceptance exceptions open until the technical reviewer signs the installation record. A carton receipt alone is insufficient for closure.

The next installation window will include a check of the sealed spare device before it is released from storage. The office helpdesk will keep the cabinet diagram beside its maintenance checklist. Staff requests for new desk connections remain separate capacity work and should not be included in this incident's repair costs. The closure record retains the original afternoon reports.
