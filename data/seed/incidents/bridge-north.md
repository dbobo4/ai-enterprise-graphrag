# Incident report — September 2026 training print queue

On 2026-09-30, Project Bridge North could not print its training handover packs. The root cause was an exhausted local print queue quota. The incident is closed. Terminal sign-in continued working, but the late shift supervisor had to postpone the final handover exercise until approved paper copies were available.

The site coordinator first checked whether the printer had paper and whether an ordinary reception document would print. A queue inspection showed a series of retained practice jobs from the rehearsal week. Staff had restarted the printer without removing the retained jobs. The team kept a list of the affected training sessions before clearing the practice queue.

Recovery released the retained practice jobs and restored the agreed queue allowance. The supervisor printed a new controlled handover pack and recorded which earlier copies were no longer valid. A successful terminal login did not close the incident because the missing output was part of the observed training task. The late shift completed the remaining exercise the following morning.

The corrective action adds a queue check at the end of every rehearsal day. Trainers must distinguish a practice print from an approved handover copy. The station guide now includes a named escalation contact for quota failures. A repeated quota problem requires an estimate of expected print volume before any permanent allowance is changed.

Operations will compare printing demand with the number of planned sessions during the first launch week. The equipment inspection found no need to replace the printer. Unused paper packs return to the training desk, where the supervisor checks the issue date. The closure record keeps the list of postponed exercises with the queue inspection results.
