# Data retention policy

Finance approves the Data retention policy. Confidential project files are retained for 24 months after project closure. Public training materials are kept for 12 months after withdrawal. The owner records the event that starts the retention period; a draft document's creation date does not start either clock.

Working copies should remain in approved team folders rather than personal mailboxes. A person sending an attachment must confirm that the recipient is entitled to receive it. Where a shared link is practical, it should point to the approved copy. Marking a document confidential does not by itself establish who may approve external distribution.

Deletion requests identify the original document, any scanned copy and the location of derived exports. The records coordinator checks whether a legal or financial hold applies before scheduling removal. If a supplier operates an export service, the service owner requests confirmation that temporary delivery copies have also been removed. A failed export does not justify retaining an untracked local copy.

The archive intake team samples scanned pages for readability and verifies that a receipt number matches the paper batch. A damaged page is quarantined with its envelope reference. Staff must not silently replace a signed document with an edited scan. Corrections are added as a dated attachment, while the approved original remains available during its retention period.

The monthly records meeting reviews unresolved deletion requests and missing closure dates. Summary figures exclude items under an active hold. A department moving to a new storage service must agree how permissions and retention dates will transfer before the move. The transfer checklist includes a small restore exercise and a named owner for any rejected batch.
