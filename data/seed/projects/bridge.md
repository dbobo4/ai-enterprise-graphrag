# Project Bridge — Employee onboarding

Tamás Fekete leads Project Bridge, the employee onboarding service, with a target go-live of 2026-12-01. The programme code is HR-22. The approved workflow collects starter details, verifies identity documents and sends a named approval request to the receiving department. Employees can complete the form before their first day.

HR checks that the employee number, start date and work location agree with the signed starter pack. A missing document pauses verification without removing the submitted information. A coordinator can record a reason for returning an application, but cannot change a signed declaration on the employee's behalf. Corrections must be acknowledged by the employee.

The pilot will include office hires and a small group joining the northern site. A welcome session does not grant permission to use production applications. The receiving department schedules equipment collection after the identity check and the employment approval are complete. HR will keep a manual appointment list if the booking screen is unavailable.

The team has reserved a quiet room for applicants who need help scanning documents. Copies containing another person's information must be rejected. Training uses sample forms and individually assigned practice accounts. The coordinator records who handled an exception so that an unresolved application can be continued by the next shift without repeating the initial interview.

Launch acceptance includes a completed starter, a returned application and an interrupted verification attempt. The helpdesk should be able to explain the current status without downloading a document image. A weekly handover will review applications waiting more than two working days. HR will compare the first month's completed starts with the signed employment register.

The receiving manager confirms the role and required equipment, while HR retains ownership of identity exceptions. A manager cannot approve their own unresolved identity check through an equipment request. Applicants who cannot complete an online step receive a booked appointment with an HR coordinator. The appointment list contains contact details and a case number, without a copy of the identity document or the reason for a verification concern.

During a verification outage, coordinators can continue checking starter packs and arranging appointments, but must not mark pending identities as approved. The support contact records the affected case numbers and asks the verification provider to confirm recovery before paused checks resume. Repeated attempts require review to avoid duplicate cases or unnecessary service charges. HR will inspect the exception queue before authorising the next onboarding group.
