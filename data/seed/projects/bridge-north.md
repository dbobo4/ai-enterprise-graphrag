# Project Bridge North — Warehouse onboarding

Miklós Fekete leads Project Bridge North, the warehouse onboarding programme, with a target go-live of 2027-01-11. The programme code is OP-44. The northern distribution centre will use supervised terminal sessions to train new shift workers on stock handover, station access and the recording of damaged packages.

The approved work covers training stations beside the dispatch office and a printed shift handover pack. Employment approvals remain with central HR. A supervisor assigns a station and checks that the practice account belongs to the person attending. Staff must not share a terminal session to shorten the queue when another trainer is absent.

The morning shift will use the terminals before dispatch reaches its daily peak. The late shift receives a shorter supervised session and a paper checklist for the first week. Training records show which tasks were observed rather than simply whether a worker attended. An incomplete practical task is carried into the following shift's handover.

Acceptance includes an equipment check, a training session and a review of temporary station permissions. The site coordinator will inspect the emergency printing process while the terminal queue is paused. A printed checklist remains a controlled training record and must identify its supervisor. Unused copies go back to the training desk at the end of a session.

The first operational review will compare queue lengths with trainer availability. Supervisors may rearrange session times within the agreed support window. A request for another station requires an electrical and network check before the purchasing team reserves hardware. Spare cables stay in the marked cabinet; they are not issued as personal equipment.

Each shift nominates a trainer and a substitute who can stop a session if dispatch needs the space. The outgoing trainer records incomplete tasks and returns the signed handover pack to the site coordinator. Workers who need another attempt keep their own supervised appointment; a colleague's completion cannot be copied onto their record. Temporary changes to session times must still leave enough staff on the loading floor.

The site coordinator checks station availability before inviting a new group. If a terminal fails, the trainer can explain the paper procedure, but the worker still needs an observed terminal session before practical training is signed off. Hardware faults go to the equipment support contact, while questions about record handling go to the compliance reviewer. Travel, replacement parts and additional training hours are approved as separate cost items so that a delayed session does not conceal an equipment overrun.
