# Project Beacon — Office network renewal

Réka Kiss leads Project Beacon, the office network renewal, with a target go-live of 2027-01-20. The programme code is PR-18. The work replaces ageing Budapest office switches, documents cable routes and introduces a signed equipment inventory. It covers permanent office connections and meeting room ports, including the reception desk.

The site survey found several desks using unlabelled patch cables. The team will label the wall ports before moving any device so that a reported fault can be traced to a cabinet position. A spare switch will remain sealed at reception until the acceptance group confirms the installed inventory. Equipment with damaged packaging is recorded before it enters the storage room.

Installation is divided into three evening windows. Staff should save open work and disconnect docking stations before leaving. The facilities coordinator will keep the cleaners away from the affected cabinets. An office printer may be unavailable during its assigned window; the training-room printer remains available for essential handover packs.

Acceptance includes a connection test from each occupied office area and a comparison of serial numbers against the delivery record. A replacement device must use approved firmware. The network engineer signs the technical check and Procurement resolves discrepancies in delivered quantities. The Finance signature confirms that charges match the approved budget allocation.

The office helpdesk will receive a short maintenance checklist and a cabinet diagram. Requests for additional desks are treated as capacity changes rather than installation defects. Staff who move seats during the rollout must notify facilities before their new port is activated. The first monthly review will check whether the spare stock is still sufficient.

Before each evening window, the installer and facilities coordinator walk the affected floor together. Cabinet keys, power availability and safe access to cable trays must be confirmed before equipment is unpacked. Reception needs a working telephone throughout the change, so its connection moves only after a temporary line has been checked. Unfinished work is isolated and labelled before the cleaning team enters the area.

Procurement will match each installed device to a delivery item and retain the supplier's warranty confirmation. Replaced switches stay in a secured cabinet until the network engineer approves disposal of their saved configuration. The cost review separates replacement hardware from additional cabling requested by departments. An unexpected cable defect can use the agreed contingency only after the site engineer records the affected route and repair estimate.
