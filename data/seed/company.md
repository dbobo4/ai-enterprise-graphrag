# HelioWorks Kft. — Operating plan, November 2026

HelioWorks Kft. has five departments: IT, HR, Procurement, Finance and Operations. The Budapest office develops internal digital services for the office and the northern distribution centre. The current investment programme covers support ticketing, employee onboarding, office networking, records migration, warehouse onboarding and a finance network sandbox.

The management committee meets on the first Tuesday of each month. Department heads approve staff allocations before the programme office reserves shared deployment windows. Changes that require weekend support must include an operations contact and an agreed rollback window. The programme office maintains separate cost centres for delivery work and continuing service charges.

The Budapest office has 86 permanent desks and two shared training rooms. The northern site works two shifts, with a smaller administrative team available after 16:00. Paper delivery receipts still accompany some shipments, so a temporary interruption of office applications does not automatically suspend warehouse dispatch. Local supervisors decide whether a manual process can continue safely.

Finance closes monthly reporting on the fourth working day. Procurement checks whether supplier invoices match approved service periods and purchase orders. An invoice can cover several service lines, but project teams must record the allocation before approval. Staff travel, catering and printed materials use separate expense categories from contracted technology services.

The January planning session will review staffing after the winter deployment period. No additional production system has been approved for the finance sandbox. The distribution centre may request another terminal station after its first month of operation, subject to an equipment inspection and a revised support estimate.
