# HelioWorks Kft. — Company profile

HelioWorks Kft. is a fictional Hungarian company for a GraphRAG course project. Its departments are IT, HR, Procurement and Finance. The organisation develops internal digital services and works with specialist technology vendors. The corpus is entirely synthetic and contains no real employee or customer data.

Documents cover projects, people, supplier relationships, incident investigations and internal policies. Statements belong to their own source documents; no single company-wide table contains every fact.
