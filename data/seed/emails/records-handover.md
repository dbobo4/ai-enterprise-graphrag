# Email — Closing temporary export folders

Date: 2026-11-08. From: Records coordination. To: Department document owners. Subject: Ownership checks for temporary delivery copies.

Please review temporary delivery folders before confirming a document transfer is complete. The owner should identify the original record, the approved destination and any copy created for inspection. A successful upload does not mean that an operator's local working copy has been removed. Ask the service owner for the provider's deletion confirmation when the delivery process used supplier-operated temporary storage.

Do not close a deletion request while a financial or legal hold remains unresolved. The records coordinator will keep the request open with a named reviewer and the reason for the hold. A scanned signature page should stay attached to its approved document. If a correction is needed, record a dated attachment rather than silently replacing the original scan.

The upcoming monthly meeting will review items with missing closure dates. Please bring the event that starts the retention period, rather than the date a draft was first created. A withdrawn public training pack and a closed confidential project file have different retention rules. Owners should also check whether obsolete printed copies remain in shared training rooms.

The inspection cabinet is almost full, so clear completed review bundles before sending another batch to the scanning desk. Unreadable pages and packages awaiting an owner should remain in separate marked trays. The dispatch counter is not an approved storage location. Return the room key to reception after the final afternoon inspection.
