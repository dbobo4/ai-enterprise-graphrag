# Email — Northern training rota

Date: 2026-11-05. From: Miklós Fekete, Operations. To: Northern shift supervisors. Subject: Supervisors for terminal practice.

Please nominate a supervisor and a substitute for each training shift. A session should record the station used and the tasks observed. A signed attendance sheet is still required, but attendance alone does not show that a worker can complete the handover process. Carry unfinished tasks into the next shift's note rather than marking the whole session complete.

The practice accounts are individual. A late arrival must wait for the assigned account instead of using the person ahead in the queue. If a station is unavailable, record its number and ask the site coordinator whether the remaining session can proceed. The emergency printed checklist must identify the supervisor and carry the current issue date.

The audit visit requires a site escort. Please confirm who can meet the visitor at reception and walk them back after the station review. Interview notes should stay in the approved folder. A photograph of an employee's identity document is not an appropriate attachment to a station-permission exception.

Facilities will inspect the spare cable cabinet on Tuesday morning. Return unused cables before that visit and keep the training desk clear for the afternoon handover. Requests for another station should include the expected queue length and the proposed location so that the electrical and network checks can be arranged together.
