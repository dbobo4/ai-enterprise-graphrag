# Email — November service invoice allocation

Date: 2026-11-07. From: Bence Molnár, Finance. To: Procurement and service owners. Subject: Separating service charges from change work.

Please check the service period on each invoice before submitting an allocation. Hosting, monitoring, storage and equipment work should remain separate lines even when they relate to the same delivery window. A technical incident reference helps explain a charge, but it does not replace the approved commercial schedule or identify who authorised an additional service.

The monthly pack needs an accepted purchase order, the delivery evidence and the cost-centre allocation. Where an acceptance exception remains open, attach the current exception status. A reception signature confirms that a carton arrived; it does not establish that its serial number, firmware and location have passed the technical inspection.

For verification charges, distinguish completed attempts from interrupted requests that were submitted again after a timeout. The employee's application number and the supplier request identifier describe different records. Please do not remove one merely because the other appears twice. HR should confirm the exception before Finance requests a correction to an invoice count.

We close the allocation pack on the fourth working day. Late estimates should include the reason they were not available at the normal cutoff. Catering, room bookings and travel remain in their own expense categories. The next subscription review will also examine unused practice accounts and service lines whose approved support window has not been used.
